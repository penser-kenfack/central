# AI Imagines the 2030 World Cup Final (Short)

Format: vertical **9:16**, about 50 seconds, 7 clips. Use one of Kling, Google Veo (Gemini / Flow), Runway or Hailuo.
All players are fictional. Don't add real player names to the prompts: the apps may refuse, and fake footage of real people can get the video taken down.

## How to make it

1. Generate each clip below. Set the aspect ratio to **9:16** and the length to **5–8 seconds**. Paste the prompt exactly.
2. If a clip looks bad (extra legs, blurry ball, warped faces), generate it again. Most apps give you several tries.
3. Join the clips in order in CapCut, add `voiceover.wav`, then use **Auto captions**. Or send me the clips and I'll put it together.

Every prompt ends with the same style line so the clips match:
`Cinematic, photorealistic, shot on a broadcast sports camera, shallow depth of field, dramatic stadium lighting, vertical 9:16 framing.`
